# BIS015L_W2026_Group6

##MICROBES IN THE ENVIRONMENT DATA PROVIDED GRACIOUSLY BY PROFESSOR MIRRIAM MARKUM, LAB PROCEDURE AND METHODOLOGY IS ALL LISTED IN MIC 104L LABRATORY MANUAL##

The code found within this repository was derived from the investigation and plating of microbes in the environment, more specifically within the indoor facilities of the Katherine Esau Hall and the outside area around it. 

Students within class sections were advised to expose either an SDA plate with Ampicillin or a NA plate with Cycloheximide for a designated amount of time in a location of their choosing. The purpose of the experiment was to map the sort of Microbes readily in the air and bring awareness and importance of Aseptic Techniques among future Microbiologist.

SDA plates were made with the expectation of fungal growth, the addition of Ampicillin was supposed to inhibit growth of bacteria and Dextrose sugar was added to plates to give Fungi and advantage as they readily can digest dextrose. NA plates were made with the expectation of growing bacteria, the addition of Cycloheximide was supposed to inhibit the growth of fungi and regular glucose was added to NA plates, the smaller size and simple structures of bacteria would mean they would outcompete any fungi growing in the plates.

Exposure time, number of bacterial/fungal looking colonies, total number of colonies found within the plate, location of the plate, the class section that recorded the data, and the selective factors and media found within plates.

2023, 2024, and 2026's data were the years the data were sampled, other years were ommitted due to having added or missing elements that made the data unfair to compare.
